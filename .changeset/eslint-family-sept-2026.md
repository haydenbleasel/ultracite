Update the ESLint family to latest: eslint 10.9.1, typescript-eslint 8.69.0, eslint-plugin-unicorn 74, eslint-plugin-solid 0.17, eslint-plugin-jsdoc 64.3.5, eslint-plugin-react-doctor and oxlint-plugin-react-doctor 0.9.13, globals 17.12, and the angular, nestjs-typed, next, tanstack-query, cypress, html, jest and storybook plugins. The dynamic presets pick up the new rules automatically: the solid preset gains the eleven rules added in eslint-plugin-solid 0.16 and 0.17 (`removed-api`, `no-single-arg-create-effect`, `no-accessor-as-prop`, `valid-use-server`, `require-async-server-function`, `no-invalid-server-capture`, `no-browser-globals-in-server-function`, `no-module-scope-reactive-primitive`, `no-restated-default-options`, `prefer-onSettled-for-side-effects`, `prefer-structured-class`), which only report Solid 2.0 issues when `settings.solid.version` is set to 2 and stay silent on Solid 1.x code; the nestjs preset gains `forward-ref-injection-should-use-wrapper-type`, `swagger-file-upload-should-be-documented` and `uploaded-file-should-be-validated`. The curated jsdoc and react-doctor lists are unchanged. No enabled rule was removed or deprecated, and the ESLint/oxlint parity check still passes.
