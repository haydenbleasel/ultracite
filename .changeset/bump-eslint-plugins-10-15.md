Update the ESLint packages `init` installs: typescript-eslint 8.73, `eslint-plugin-import-x` 4.18.1, `eslint-plugin-solid` 0.20, `@angular-eslint/eslint-plugin`, `@angular-eslint/eslint-plugin-template` and `@angular-eslint/template-parser` 22.8, `eslint-plugin-svelte` 3.24.1, `eslint-plugin-vue` 10.12.1, `eslint-plugin-jsdoc` 65.4.12, `eslint-plugin-storybook` 10.6.7, `@tanstack/eslint-plugin-query` 5.108, `@next/eslint-plugin-next` 16.4.6, `eslint-plugin-cypress` 7.0.5, `eslint-plugin-jest` 29.17.1, `eslint-plugin-playwright` 2.12.2, `eslint-plugin-qwik` 1.20.4 and `@darraghor/eslint-plugin-nestjs-typed` 7.5.10. The presets were checked against ESLint 10.15, and the `eslint` peer range stays at `^10.9.0`.

New rules the presets turn on:

- `ultracite/eslint/type-aware`: [`@typescript-eslint/no-unsafe-class-instance-assignment`](https://typescript-eslint.io/rules/no-unsafe-class-instance-assignment) reports an object literal, or another value no constructor made, used where an instance of a class is expected, such as `const point: Point = { x: 1, y: 2 }`.
- Angular: [`@angular-eslint/no-global-document`](https://github.com/angular-eslint/angular-eslint/blob/main/packages/eslint-plugin/docs/rules/no-global-document.md) reports the global `document` in a component, directive, service or pipe, where server-side rendering has none. In templates, [`no-multi-token-class-key`](https://github.com/angular-eslint/angular-eslint/blob/main/packages/eslint-plugin-template/docs/rules/no-multi-token-class-key.md) reports a `[class]` object key with a space in it, which Angular drops, and [`prefer-let-for-async-pipe`](https://github.com/angular-eslint/angular-eslint/blob/main/packages/eslint-plugin-template/docs/rules/prefer-let-for-async-pipe.md) reports the same `async` pipe expression used twice in one view. `prefer-style-binding` also gets its new `bindUnits` option, so ``[style.height]="`${height()}em`"`` is reported in favour of `[style.height.em]="height()"`.
- Solid: `solid/validate-jsx-nesting` reports JSX the HTML parser restructures, such as `<p><div /></p>`. `solid/action-yield-after-await` asks for a bare `yield` after each `await` in a generator passed to Solid 2.0's `action()`. `solid/no-unassigned-vars` is the core `no-unassigned-vars` with `ref={el}` counted as an assignment; the Solid preset now turns the core rule off in `.jsx` and `.tsx` files, where it reported every `let el; <div ref={el} />`.

`eslint-plugin-import-x` 4.18 adds four rules about barrel files, and the preset leaves all of them off to keep the decisions it already has: `avoid-barrel-files` and `avoid-re-export-all` report what a package's entry point has to do, `avoid-namespace-import` reports the `import * as` that `no-namespace` allows, and `avoid-importing-barrel-files` is a different check from the one the Oxlint preset runs.

Some rules that were already on behave differently. `solid/removed-api`, which the Solid preset has on for every project, reports more Solid 1.x APIs that 2.0 removed (`createDynamic`, `<Ctx.Provider>` and imports from `solid-js/h`, `solid-js/html` and `solid-js/universal`), and `ultracite fix` no longer rewrites `onMount`, `batch`, `mergeProps` or `unwrap` to their 2.0 replacements, which behave differently. `solid/reactivity` again reports a stale capture in a handler declared as a statement. `eslint-plugin-import-x` rewrote its module analysis: `no-cycle` now reports cycles through side-effect imports, and many `no-duplicates`, `extensions` and `order` fixes were corrected. ESLint 10.15 makes `func-names` and `accessor-pairs` check a few more forms.
